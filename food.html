Beef Tartare

Food Description


Beef tartare is a raw ground beef dish, a classic French appetizer often served with onions, capers, parsley, and Worcestershire sauce. It is typically seasoned and topped with a raw egg yolk. The dish's origin is debated, with some suggesting it has European roots, possibly in France or Russia.
